Who We Are

NOVA & CO. is a fictional clothing brand created for a Web Designing assignment. Our website presents different types of clothing in a simple and organized way.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide information about comfortable, stylish and affordable everyday clothing. We aim to make our website easy to understand and use.
